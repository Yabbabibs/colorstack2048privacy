Privacy Policy

Color Stack 2048 · Android and iOS · app ID com.TigerTiger.TubeMerge
Effective date: 1 October 2026 · Last updated: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TigerTiger ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Color Stack 2048 (the "Game") on Android and iOS. By installing or playing the Game you agree to the practices described here. Where the two platforms differ, each section says which one it is talking about.

1. The short version

We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number or postal address, and we do not run servers of our own - the Game's online features run on Unity Gaming Services on our behalf. Your progress is saved on your device and, if you are signed in to your platform's game service, also backed up to that service's cloud save so you can restore it. Some information is collected by the services the Game is built on - Google AdMob for advertising on both platforms; Google Play Games Services and Google Play billing on Android; Apple Game Center, iCloud and the App Store on iOS; and, for signed-in players, Unity Gaming Services, which hosts the Game's leaderboards and its social features (friends, player profiles and watching a friend's game) - and this policy explains what those are.

2. Information collected

2.1 Your game progress

Your game progress - score, gold, gems, unlocked characters, levels, quests, settings, sound and vibration preferences, and tutorial state - is saved on your device, and we cannot read it.

If you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), a copy of that progress is also kept in your account's cloud save - Google Play Games Services on Android, Apple's iCloud on iOS - so you can restore it after reinstalling the Game or on a new device. The Game signs you in automatically when your device is already signed in to Play Games or Game Center; it is fully playable without either, and you can sign out in the Play Games app or in your iPhone's Game Center settings.

The copy on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game. If your device's own backup (Android backup or iCloud Backup) is switched on, that backup may also contain it; that backup belongs to your Google or Apple account and is under your control, not ours.

2.2 Social features (signed-in players only)

If you are signed in to Play Games or Game Center, the Game's social features are available. They work through Unity Gaming Services and involve the following information:

  • Your public profile: your Play Games or Game Center name with a short tag, the in-game character you picked as your picture, your title and account level, a character you pin, and your best scores. Any signed-in player of the Game can see this card.
  • Profile details and recent games: play statistics (such as runs played, merges, characters owned, puzzles solved), your favourite characters, milestones you reached and your most recent runs. These are shown only to your friends, or to every player if you set your profile to public. Our server code enforces this; other players cannot read them directly.
  • Friends: your friends list, the friend requests you send and receive, and the players you block. Friend requests you have not answered expire after 15 days.
  • Online status: whether you are online and, while you play, the game mode, the character and the current score of your run, so that your friends can see you are playing. You can hide this by choosing to appear offline.
  • Watching: when a friend watches your game, a snapshot of that run and its moves are sent through Unity's servers to that friend while they watch, through a temporary room that is deleted when the run ends or the last watcher leaves, together with the reactions (emoji) they send you. You can see who is watching and mute them.
  • Cheers and invites: the cheers you give to friends' news, and the invite code you share, including which players joined through it, so that invite rewards can be granted.

None of this is sent if you are not signed in. We do not use it for advertising and do not share it with anyone other than Unity, which stores it for us, and the other players it is shown to as described above.

2.4 What we never collect

The Game does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera input, precise GPS location, health data, or any financial account information. The Game contains no third-party analytics SDK and does not connect to any social network (such as Facebook); its friends feature is its own, run on Unity Gaming Services, and it never reads your phone's contacts.

3. Advertising and your choices

Ads in the Game are served by Google AdMob. Depending on your region, your device settings and, on iOS, your answer to Apple's tracking prompt, ads may be personalised (based on your device's advertising identifier) or non-personalised.

  • Android: the advertising ID is a setting of your phone, not of the Game. In your device settings (usually Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads on older versions) you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation. Turning it off does not remove ads, but the ads you see will no longer be based on your advertising ID.
  • iOS: the Game can only use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow it in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose not to allow tracking, or are never asked, the IDFA is not shared and the ads you see are not based on it. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (both platforms): the Game uses Google's consent form (a certified consent management platform). No ad is requested until you have answered it, and your choice decides whether the ads you see may be personalised. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Privacy in the Game.
  • Removing ads: buying the Starter Pack permanently removes the timed full-screen ads shown between runs. Rewarded ads are not affected: they stay optional and appear only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward.

If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, please contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate steps.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: deleted when you uninstall the Game (or, on Android, clear its app data). A device backup you have switched on is managed through your Google or Apple account.
  • Cloud save and achievements - Android: held by Google Play Games Services under your Google account. You can delete this data yourself at myaccount.google.com or from the Play Games app under Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  • Cloud save and achievements - iOS: held by Apple under your Apple Account, in iCloud and Game Center. You can remove the Game's iCloud data from your iPhone's iCloud storage settings, and request a copy or deletion of your Apple data at privacy.apple.com.
  • Leaderboard entries: held by Unity Gaming Services in the Game's own project. Weekly leaderboards are cleared every week; all-time leaderboards are kept for as long as the Game runs them. Deleting your Google or Apple data does not remove these entries - to have your Unity player record and your scores deleted, email us at the address in section 9 and we will delete them.
  • Social data (your profile, friends list, requests, blocks, invites and cheers): held by Unity Gaming Services in the Game's own project for as long as your Unity player record exists. Your online status is updated live and not kept as a history. When a friend starts watching you, a snapshot of your current run is stored so they can catch up; it is replaced by the next one and deleted with your Unity player record. Deleting your Unity player record, as above, deletes your social data with it.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google according to Google's own retention policies; reset or delete your advertising ID, or withdraw tracking permission, as described in section 3.
  • Purchase records: retained by Google Play or the App Store as part of your account's purchase history.

Apart from leaderboard entries and social data, we do not hold a copy of your data ourselves, so other deletion requests are best made through the Google and Apple controls above. If you need help, write to us and we will point you to the right place.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data relating to you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent - for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Brazil's LGPD or Turkey's KVKK. We do not sell personal information for money. Where personalised ads are shown, Google AdMob's use of your advertising identifier may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CCPA; you can opt out of that at any time by turning off ad personalisation (Android) or not allowing tracking (iOS), as described in section 3. To exercise a right, contact us at the address in section 9; we will handle leaderboard and social data ourselves, and where the data is held by Google or Apple we will tell you which of their controls applies.

7. Security

Data transmitted between the Game and Google's, Apple's and Unity's services is protected in transit by industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
