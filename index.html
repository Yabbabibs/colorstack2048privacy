Privacy Policy

Color Stack 2048 · Android package com.TigerTiger.TubeMerge
Effective date: 26 August 2026 · Last updated: 26 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TigerTiger ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Color Stack 2048 (the "Game"). By installing or playing the Game you agree to the practices described here.

1. The short version

We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number or postal address, and we do not run servers that store your personal data. The Game keeps your progress on your own device. Some information is nevertheless collected by the Google services the Game is built on - advertising, Google Play Games Services and Google Play billing - and this policy explains what those are.

2. Information collected

2.1 Data stored on your device

Your game progress - score, gold, gems, unlocked characters, levels, quests, settings, sound and vibration preferences, and tutorial state - is saved locally on your device. It stays on your device, is not readable by us, and is removed when you uninstall the Game.

2.2 Data collected by third-party services

ServiceWhat it may collectWhy
Google AdMob (advertising) Advertising ID, IP address, device and OS information, coarse (non-precise) location derived from IP, and interactions with ads shown in the Game. To display interstitial and rewarded video ads, limit how often the same ad is shown, measure ad performance and detect invalid traffic.
Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in) Your Play Games player ID and public gamer profile name, your leaderboard scores, achievements and cloud-saved game progress. Google Play in-app review and in-app updates Standard Google Play app and device information. To offer an in-game rating prompt and to tell you when a newer version is available.

These services act as independent data controllers for the data they collect. Their handling of that data is governed by Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy (see also how Google uses data from AdMob partner apps).

2.3 What we never collect

The Game does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera input, precise GPS location, health data, or any financial account information. The Game contains no third-party analytics SDK and no social network SDK.

3. Advertising and your choices

Ads in the Game are served by Google AdMob. Depending on your region and your settings, ads may be personalised (based on the advertising ID associated with your device) or non-personalised.

  • Android: open Settings → Google → Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID, or to turn off ad personalisation entirely. Turning it off does not remove ads, but the ads you see will no longer be based on your advertising ID.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: where required, a Google-provided consent form is shown before personalised ads are used, and your choice can be changed at any time from the Game's settings screen where that option is available.
  • You can also remove ads by purchasing the relevant in-app product, where offered.

4. Children

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, please contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate steps.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Local save data: deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its app data.
  • Cloud save, leaderboard scores and achievements: held by Google Play Games Services under your Google account. You can delete this data yourself at myaccount.google.com or from the Play Games app under Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google according to Google's own retention policies; reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 3.
  • Purchase records: retained by Google Play as part of your Google account order history.

Because we do not hold a copy of your data ourselves, requests to delete data are best made through the Google controls above. If you need help, write to us and we will point you to the right place.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data relating to you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent - for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Brazil's LGPD or Turkey's KVKK. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising in the sense given to those terms by the CCPA. To exercise a right, contact us at the address in section 9; where the data is held by Google, we will tell you which Google control applies.

7. Security

Data transmitted between the Game and Google's services is protected in transit by industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
